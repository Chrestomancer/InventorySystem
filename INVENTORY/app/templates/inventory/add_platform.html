About Sales Platforms

Sales platforms are the marketplaces where you list and sell your inventory items. Adding platforms to the system allows you to:

  • Track which platform each listing is on
  • Calculate platform-specific fees automatically
  • Compare performance across different sales channels
  • Generate platform-specific reports
Common Platform Fee Percentages
Platform Typical Fee Percentage Notes
eBay 10-12% Varies by category
Amazon 15% Varies by category, plus subscription fees
Etsy 6.5% Plus listing fees
Craigslist 0% Free for most categories
Facebook Marketplace 0% Free for personal sales

Note: These are approximate values and may change. Always check the platform's current fee structure.
