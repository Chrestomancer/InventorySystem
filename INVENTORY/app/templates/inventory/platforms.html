About Sales Platforms

Sales platforms are the marketplaces where you list and sell your inventory items. Configuring platforms allows you to:

  • Track which platform each listing is on
  • Calculate platform-specific fees automatically
  • Compare performance across different sales channels
  • Generate platform-specific reports

For each platform, you can specify:

  • Name: The name of the platform (e.g., eBay, Craigslist, Amazon)
  • URL: The website URL for the platform (optional)
  • Fee Percentage: The standard fee percentage charged by the platform

When you create a listing, you'll select which platform it's listed on, and when you record a sale, the system will automatically calculate the platform fee based on the percentage you've configured.
