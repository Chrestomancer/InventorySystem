About Inventory Management System

A comprehensive solution for tracking inventory, managing listings across multiple sales platforms, and analyzing sales performance.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide resellers and small business owners with a powerful yet easy-to-use inventory management system that helps them track their inventory, manage sales across multiple platforms, and make data-driven decisions to maximize profitability.

We understand the challenges of managing inventory across multiple sales channels, tracking costs and profits, and staying organized. Our system is designed to address these challenges and help you grow your business.

Key Features

  • Inventory Tracking: Keep track of your inventory with detailed product information, quantities, and locations.
  • Multi-Platform Sales: Manage listings across multiple sales platforms like eBay, Craigslist, and more.
  • Sales Analytics: Analyze your sales performance with detailed reports and visualizations.
  • Profit Calculation: Automatically calculate profit margins based on costs, fees, and sale prices.
  • Time in Stock Tracking: Monitor how long items have been in inventory.

How It Works

Add Inventory

Add your items to the inventory with details like cost, condition, and quantity.

Create Listings

Create listings for your items on various sales platforms.

Record Sales

Record sales transactions with details on final price, fees, and taxes.

Analyze Data

Generate reports to analyze sales performance and make informed decisions.

Benefits

  • Save Time: Streamline your inventory management process and reduce manual work.
  • Increase Accuracy: Reduce errors in tracking inventory and calculating profits.
  • Make Better Decisions: Use data-driven insights to optimize your business.
  • Improve Cash Flow: Identify slow-moving inventory and optimize pricing.
  • Scale Your Business: Manage more inventory and sales channels without increasing workload.
